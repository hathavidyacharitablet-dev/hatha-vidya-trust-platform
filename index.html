About Our Trust

Hatha Vidya Charitable Trust, based in Gandhidham, Kutch, Gujarat, is committed to serving society through compassion, responsibility and community participation.

Our aim is to support initiatives that contribute to education, social welfare, health awareness, environmental care and the well-being of living beings.
